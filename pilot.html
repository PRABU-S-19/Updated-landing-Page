What happens next

  1. We read your submission and respond from humanresource@aerovanttech.com within 1 business day.
  2. Quick 20-minute call to understand your hiring workflow and align on success metrics.
  3. We provision your pilot workspace with 10 candidate assessment credits.

  5. Two weeks in, we review what worked and what didn't. No-obligation decision.

How we handle candidate data

Stored in India (AWS Mumbai), encrypted in transit and at rest. Scoring text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which does not train on it. See the security page and DPA.

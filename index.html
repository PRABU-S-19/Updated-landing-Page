They are three stages of one journey. The Lab tests how you reason through an attack on a real architecture. Lab Simulations are role-based tasks that mirror daily security work. InterviewPrep builds an interview from a job description and your resume. All three use the same five-dimension rubric.

No. Every role track starts at Beginner and the free plan covers Beginner on any role. Lab Simulations are designed for freshers and final-year students.

Each answer goes through three AI passes — Evaluator, Challenger, Reconciler — against the published rubric. When passes disagree significantly, the score carries a confidence flag. Read a score as evidence to review alongside the transcript, not a verdict.

Challenge it within 14 days by emailing admin@aerovanttech.com with your session ID. A security engineer reviews the transcript and rubric application within 7 business days and replies with a confirmed or revised score. Full process.

Production data is stored in India (AWS Mumbai), encrypted in transit and at rest. Assessment text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for scoring, which does not train on it. Raw scoring outputs are kept 90 days for audit. GDPR and India DPDPA 2023 rights are supported. See the privacy policy and security page.
